Dear Place Map

Mark the places in the Seattle area where you feel nostalgic, uneasy, or comfortable.

How it works

  1. Choose a question below. You can place one mark for each.
  2. Tap the map once to place it. To move it, drag the mark or choose it again and tap a new spot.
  3. Tell us why. What happens there that makes you feel this way?
  4. Tap "Send my map". Your email app opens with your map. Just tap send.
